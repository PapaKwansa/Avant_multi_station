# M1 geological compartment Bayesian inversion (synthetic, nine parameters)

This is the bounded Bayesian-inference follow-up to the **committed M1 synthetic MAP** run on the `feature/full-hydromechanical-spatial-inversion` branch, commit `3ce48f0`. It **adds** files and does not overwrite the production hydraulic solver, verified Larry strain kernel, or previous DREAM implementation.

## Scientific scope

This samples the nine-parameter vector

`[log10_K_inside, log10_K_outside, log10_Ss, log10_gamma_rel, a_m, b_m, theta_deg, x0_prime_m, y0_prime_m]`.

All nine parameters are **sampled**. None is fixed to its synthetic generating value. The source rectangle is a hydraulic **conductivity contrast**, not a pressure-loading cutoff. The fixed, separately defined pressure-loading support and existing homogeneous-elastic cuboidal kernel are retained. Hydraulic pressure is computed using the current one-way two-zone FV model.

The posterior density is proportional to a training-only joint Gaussian pressure/strain likelihood times the stated independent bounded priors. The likelihood uses the **immutable** `m1_synthetic_observations.npz` generated by `run_m1_synthetic_map.py`; it **never generates fresh observations** during DREAM and never uses held-out S07/S08 or the third synthetic gauge in fitting. For each candidate, the same FV pressure field predicts pressure and strain. Gamma is an exact amplitude multiplier; the per-worker cache avoids repeating FV solves when only gamma changes.

**Important:** `m1_bayesian_priors_001.json` contains *illustrative*, deliberately non-oracle prior centers and widths. The Gaussian prior means for outside K, `b_m` and `y0_prime_m` are **not independently measured geology** and do **not** equal generating truth. These are synthetic-assumption demonstrations. Replace such priors with actual, documented geological measurements and justified uncertainty **before any site-specific interpretation**. Editing the prior JSON creates a different experiment with its own SHA provenance.

The prior uses bounded uniform or normalized bounded Gaussian (`scipy.stats.truncnorm`) distributions; PyDREAM evaluates the prior, while the worker evaluates **only** the likelihood. This avoids double-counting the prior.

## Acceptance / stop criteria

- The preflight must exactly reproduce the training likelihood from the saved full M1 MAP, without touching held-out data.
- All tested forward responses must remain finite. Legacy intermediate `arctan` warnings must be investigated if observables are not finite or perturbation-stable.
- Four or more independently initialized chains, rank-folded split R-hat < **1.01**, bulk AND tail ESS >= **400** for **every** parameter, and >=100 post-burn samples per chain are required by the software's diagnostic screen. Diagnostics are necessary, not sufficient to rule out missed modes.
- Compare posterior spread and correlations for `K_outside`, `b_m`, `theta_deg`, `x0_prime_m`, `y0_prime_m` and the `Ss`/gamma coupling; test whether apparent precision comes from priors rather than the pressure/strain likelihood.
- Use reserved stations S07/S08 and the invented gauge (0,-350) m for pointwise 90% **noisy** predictive checks; coverage of one dataset is not proof of calibrated field uncertainty.
- Check numerical resolution effects on posterior-supported geological boundaries before reporting precise map locations.
- **If sampling does not converge or boundary parameters are prior dominated, do not claim full nine-parameter boundary recovery.** Use the smaller supported subspace with physically justified geological information, or publish M1 as a forward model while retaining the validated hydraulic-inversion contribution.

## Physical limitations

* This remains **one-way** coupling: pressure affects strain but deformation does not alter pressure or storage.
* The hydraulic FV model is a confined, single-layer 2D approximation with provisional external Dirichlet boundaries, a weighted point-well approximation and coarse-grid mixed-cell effective conductivity.
* The analytical cuboidal kernel assumes homogeneous, isotropic linear elastic half-space mechanics. It does not model a mechanically contrasting inclusion, fault slip, layered elasticity, or a full 3D heterogeneity.
* `gamma_rel` is the normalized **alpha/E** coupling; `E` and `alpha` are structurally confounded in this model.
* Mechanical integration-support truncation and FV grid-size effects must be treated as part of a model-error budget; posteriors using only measurement noise may be too narrow.
* The saved synthetic observations were generated by the **same code**, with invented sensor positions. They are **not** COMSOL or field measurements. Independent coupled verification remains outstanding.

This is the bounded Bayesian completion step before decisions about scientific code freeze, figures, packaging and the paper.
